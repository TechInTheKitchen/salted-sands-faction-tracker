# The Salted Sands — faction names and contacts

Compiled 2026-10-09 from the available campaign notes. This is a DM review roster between arcs, not a declaration of current standings. Session summaries through Session 16 take precedence for actual party encounters; unplayed quest material and named leaders do not imply that the party knows or can reach them. Blank contacts mean no named contact was found. No awareness, opinion, or influence scores have been assigned.

Main rows include nations, organizations, political movements, and faith networks. Optional rows cover branches and institutions that can be tracked separately. Parent and child entries overlap intentionally. Locations, individual antagonists, deities, and unnamed hypothetical groups are not counted as independent factions.

## Boundaries and unresolved naming

- The Silvered Sword and Argent Fold are treated as venues, not independent factions: the Fold note describes the Sword like an organization, while the dedicated Sword note describes a speakeasy. Marvi “Redglint” Thal is the named venue contact if you want a separate social-network entry.
- Kessa's brokerage has no formal faction name. Kessa is an independent contact across several groups, not their appointed representative.
- Salt–Sky–Stone Faithful and the Tidelorn Trinity are grouped for now; the Trinity's unnamed outreach arm can become a separate row if it develops an identity.
- Conclave of Banners, Speaker Conclave, and Silver Court appear in overlapping Elvanian descriptions. The roster does not assert that they are identical institutions.
- The Quiet Table is the Common Weal's leadership gathering, not an additional independent faction. Haven's board and operational divisions likewise remain under Haven Securities.
- Sela Varn, Orren Vask, Latchkey, the Star-Beneath, and other individual entities are not faction rows. Planned revelations about Sela are not used to overwrite the recorded Session 16 situation.
- Old Party Standing is explicitly only current through Session 6; it has not been converted into present scores.
